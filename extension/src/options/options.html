Privacy & limits

  • Only the URL you explicitly scan is sent to your CIRS server, and only when you press Scan or Report.
  • No page content, form values, cookies, tokens or browsing history are collected.
  • Threat-intelligence lookups are performed by the server, not the browser. If no API key is configured, results are clearly marked as heuristic-only.
  • CIRS is not an antivirus. A SAFE result means no known threat was reported — it is not a guarantee.
